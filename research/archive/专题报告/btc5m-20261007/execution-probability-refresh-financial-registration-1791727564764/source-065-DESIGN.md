# Fixed probability refresh: 36 finite-account comparison

This is a pure design, not a funded replay or permission to trade. Proceed only after the corrected latest-15 first-snapshot probability source has an accepted full independent raw-source audit and nonzero support on all required causal-teacher and outer dates. Preserve the original zero-support source and any native preflight failure.

Six arms are fixed before financial outcomes: seed, carried USD tick with current market/reference, fresh USD with current market/reference, each with verified terminal holding and original market-information exit. Both directions must belong to the same three-way supported forecast mask **and have the identical original first decision timestamp and source-quote signal timestamp**. A later opposing snapshot cannot rank directions for an earlier order. Mismatched timestamps skip that source slot without hindsight rescheduling. At the identical new source quote, rank directional terminal expectation in dollars after original fee estimate and retain the original 1 percentage point times quoted shares margin. No threshold grid, new training, price bucket filter, winner/positive-fill restriction or best-in-round selection. Terminal EV is an entry feature for both exits; it is not called a resale-value prediction.

36 accounts: 6 arms × normal/stress × validation/score/continuous. $100 capital, $10 acquired inventory and pending requests, one tradable position, first positive opportunity consumes the round even if blocked, $1 amount and original 2-cent protection, actual additional 1/2-second original execution delay and fees. The known rate reservation applies for fee exponent at least 1; quoted source shares never cap future amount-order quantity. Zero, source-cancelled pending order, partial execution, partial sales, expiry and original official payout availability are distinct. Expiry frees the tradable slot without paying cash. All unknown assets get explicit bounds.

Use the already accepted fixed policy paths and the complete paper-account lifecycle; independently verify every selection and money event. Compare validation and score and continuous accounts, daily contribution, execution-conditioned probability calibration, capital occupancy, fee attribution and concentration in rare wins. All 15 historical dates are exploratory, not an untouched future test. Even a better historical net does not authorize adoption: require normal/stress robustness, original unknown-asset bounds, independent complete financial audit and actual-birthday forward paper validation. Keep all failures and main unchanged until those requirements are met.
