# Fixed seed quote residual versus original signal, finite paper accounts

The two arms share the same new16-date original seed source, first actual1s/2s delay, $1 amount and two-cent price protection. Only the entry probability changes. Both hold to original verified payment, which isolates entry effects; this is a research policy, not an update to the main market-information exit system.

All choices are committed from accepted seed source and predictions before attaching actual delayed execution or payment. Cash is $100, maximum combined reserves and inventory cost $10, one tradable position, one first positive opportunity per round with the existing resource-block disposition. Validation ends at score start; scoring and continuous accounts end at declared ended-source cutoff. Unknown inventory produces lower/upper account-value bounds, never invented payment or zero loss. No retrospective fee repricing or future fill-based position size.

Every original money event and all12 summaries need independent ledger/selector verification. Increasing prediction quality is not sufficient for adoption. Zero trades, fewer losing trades, wins without net dollar improvement, or less negative return do not certify profitability. All days, uncertainty bounds and failed arms remain available, followed by truly future simulated evidence before upgrading the strategy.
