# Conditioning on observed rolling TWAP history

The unconditional-risk teacher failed validation and remains a rejected candidate. This separate hypothesis examines the conditional mean supported by actual earlier TWAP observations. For a zero-drift, constant-variance Brownian instantaneous price and linearized rolling 60s price mean, the observed TWAP has stationary increment variance K(h). The future increment covariance with a known backward increment of length d is [K(h+d)-K(h)-K(d)]/2. Joint backward increments have covariance [K(a)+K(b)-K(|a-b|)]/2. A three-observation Gaussian conditional mean follows from solving this actual covariance matrix.

Use actual known preceding samples near 10 and 30 seconds and the oldest certified source history point, without invented one-second spacing or interpolation. Retain original availability, source clocks, weights and missing dispositions. The model conditions only on these observed TWAP increments; it does not claim to observe the underlying Chainlink instantaneous series or condition on all market information.
